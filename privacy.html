PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

Second Flight Studios respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, mobile phone number, and details about a service inquiry.

How we use information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests.
  • Provide requested services.
  • Ask follow-up questions.
  • Coordinate scheduling.
  • Send service-related updates.
  • Maintain and improve our services.

SMS privacy

If you opt in to receive SMS messages, Second Flight Studios may use your mobile phone number and consent record to send conversational messages concerning your service inquiry, follow-up questions, scheduling, and service updates.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent to receive SMS messages is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may provide information to service providers that help us operate our business or deliver messages. These providers may use the information only to perform services on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Cookies and website data

Our website may collect limited technical information, such as browser type, device type, referring pages, and general usage data. This information may be used to maintain, secure, and improve the website.

Data retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information you have provided. You may withdraw SMS consent at any time by replying STOP.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. Any revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@secondflightstudios.com .
